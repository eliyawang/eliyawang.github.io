1. Cats

I have two cats at home. The one on the left is named Nana, and the one on the right is called Lily. They were both rescued from the street, and they are both female.

Nana is a fat cat which weighs around 7.5 kilograms. She is already 7 years old and really like to eat. However, she is not very scared of people and is super friendly and affectionate.
