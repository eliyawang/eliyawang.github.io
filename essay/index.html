About "food Waste'

Compared to some of the countries in the world that face an extreme living environment, living in Taiwan is undoubtedly lucky since this island has sufficient resources, like clean water and agricultural production. However, most of the residents don’t realize how precious these resources are, which leads to a large amount of waste, especially food waste.

First, we have to define what food waste means. Food waste refers to food that is properly processed or prepared and still suitable for humans to eat but is discarded for serveral reasons.This often occurs in supermarkets, restaurants, and households. For example, in supermarkets, food is sometimes discarded because it does not meet appearance standards, even though it is still edible. When people buy too much food that they can not finish before its expiration date, people tend to throw it away. Or in restaurants, customers may leave uneaten food on their plates, which is later discarded by the staff.

In Taiwan, about 4.05 million tons of food are wasted every year. On average, each person wastes around 170 kilograms of food annually, which is like throwing away one lunchbox every day. When we waste food, it also means that all the resources used to produce that food are wasted as well, which result in significant economic losses and substantial environmental costs.

In conclusion, food waste is both a personal and environmental issue. Although it may seem like a small action when individuals throw away food, the accumulated impact is extremely large. Therefore, everyone should be more aware of food waste and try to reduce it in daily life, such as buying only what is needed and not wasting food.
